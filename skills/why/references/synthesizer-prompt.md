# Synthesis checklist

This checklist belongs to the current thread. Do not create a separate synthesizer.

1. Read every finding and reconcile duplicate references to the same commit, PR, issue, or document.
2. Verify citations that support a material conclusion. Confirm that each source says what the finding claims.
3. Classify claims with `epistemics.md`. Keep direct evidence separate from inference.
4. Preserve contradictions. Do not choose one account merely because it makes a cleaner story.
5. Compare the leading explanation with plausible alternatives and state what supports or weakens each one.
6. Name unanswered questions and unsuccessful searches. Do not fill gaps with likely-sounding intent.

Answer the user's question first. Add only the sections needed for direct findings, inferences, competing explanations, gaps, and sources checked. End with a short confidence statement.

If the investigation informs a planned change, extract four constraints:

- **Preserve:** behavior or intent the change must retain
- **Change:** outdated assumptions the change may replace
- **Avoid:** rejected approaches that still fail for documented reasons
- **Risk:** uncertain history that needs verification during implementation
