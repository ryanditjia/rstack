# prove it works

Inspect the real artifact or exercise the changed feature path. Compilation, timestamps, cached screenshots, and delegate summaries do not prove behavior.

For integrations, check the relevant input-to-output path. If observation gives implausible results, verify the observation method. Inspect delegated artifacts yourself.

Use a rerunnable check when it improves proof; retain it when future verification needs it. Scale verification to risk and report what remains unverified instead of substituting a proxy.
