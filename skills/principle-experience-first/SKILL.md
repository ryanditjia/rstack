# experience first

Choose the target from the user's central workflow. Every feature, option, and control must earn its place. Prefer fewer polished capabilities when scope threatens quality.

Prototype uncertain interactions before committing. Include feedback, errors, transitions, and accessibility in the experience. Library consumers and maintainers are users too. Explain consequences from their perspective; implementation convenience alone does not settle the choice.
